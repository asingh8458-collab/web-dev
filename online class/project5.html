Biography & Unique Path

Bridging Human Psychology & Tech

Hi, I'm Aakrantee Singh, born and raised in Nepal, with an academic and professional background that uniquely bridges human psychology and modern technology. My original path began in mental health, earning my license as a professional psychosocial counselor. Working closely with human emotions gave me deep empathy, but my natural curiosity urged me to explore completely unfamiliar horizons. Driven to step out of my comfort zone, I took a bold leap into technology by enrolling in a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) program. Blending counseling rigor with software logic provides me a unique lens on human-computer interaction.

Journey & Motivation

Transitioning into computer science from a non-technical background was certainly not a walk in the park. Facing abstract algorithms and coding obstacles can be daunting. However, whenever self-doubt kicks in, I make a conscious choice to motivate myself, remember how far I have come, and reignite the innate curiosity that pushed me to start this degree.
